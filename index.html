Selected Research Projects

Postdoctoral Projects (Bioinspired Materials)

  • Structural color biomimetics: Large-scale chitin-based materials mimicking butterfly wing iridescence for pigment-free color coatings.
  • Water-resistant chitin biocomposites: Chitin–Ni biocomposites with enhanced mechanical strength in wet conditions (stronger when wet).
  • Ultra-low-cost bioink: Fully biocompatible, low-cost resistive bioink for non-invasive monitoring and biomechanical sensing.
  • Mechanically graded chitin–inorganic sheets: Single-layer composites with spatially tunable stiffness for flexible devices.
  • Biocompatible water-resistant foams: Chitin-based foams as sustainable alternatives to polyurethane medical foams.
  • Bioinspired surgical bio-adhesive: Skin-compatible, wet-adhesive material inspired by aquatic organisms for surgical use.
  • UV-blocking, heat-resistant greenhouse roof: Chitin–shellac biocomposites for biodegradable, UV-blocking, heat-resistant greenhouse roofing.

Ph.D. Projects (Functional Oxide Materials)

  • Rare-earth-doped TiO2 photocatalysts with enhanced defect-engineered activity.
  • Self-powered TiO2
  • Prismoid defect-engineered ZnO with soft ferromagnetism at room temperature.
  • Zn/Cd/Mn–SnO3 ternary metal oxide gas sensors for H2S and N-containing gases.
